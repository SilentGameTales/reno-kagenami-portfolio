Narrative design / album brief

An unfolding encounter.

A late-night driver hears a strange transmission. The voice on the frequency changes, pulling him toward an entity that wants control of two souls. Each song advances this same supernatural encounter instead of operating as a disconnected single.

Production methods

A unified visual language.

Visual direction

Ruined cathedral architecture, dark wood, spectral imagery, hard shadows, and dramatic red light create a cohesive album identity. Each frame is selected for its distinct camera angle and progression through the environment.

Vocal & sonic direction

The eight-track sequence was planned around male leads, VEXA solos, and duets. A recurring motif and increasingly tense sound design were part of the concept brief; their audible presence has not been formally measured.

Technical evidence

The original uploaded audio files total 33:44.88 at 44.1 kHz stereo PCM 16-bit. Measured sample peaks ranged roughly from −0.16 to −0.09 dBFS. This verifies the supplied files, not mastering quality.

Evaluation still to do

Detailed voice-style comparison, sound-motif continuity, mix-review scoring and image consistency across small details remain candidates for later documented evaluation. No fabricated performance metrics are presented.

Credits / attribution

Direction and generation.

This is a self-initiated creative project. The creator established the horror narrative, vocal casting sequence, album mood and visual direction, selected assets and organized the campaign. Generative AI tools supported music, voice, and visual production. No human-sung recording or released animated music video is claimed.

Deliverables: eight finished audio files, the official album cover, and six approved promotional stills. Future case-study improvements will document per-track prompt history and formal evaluation results where source evidence exists.
