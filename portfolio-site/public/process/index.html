Production method & professional focus

Creative systems.
Not just outputs.

I use AI as a production tool within a creator-led workflow: define an outcome, build its constraints, generate possibilities, review against criteria, and deliver finished material with honest attribution.

Method

A clear four-part process.

01 / INTENT

Creative brief

Define narrative intent, audience, technical format, established lore, voice or character identity, and excluded outcomes.

02 / PRODUCTION

Generate & assemble

Develop AI-assisted text, music, images, character designs, visual sequences, and production-ready references.

03 / ASSURANCE

Review & compare

Check continuity across media, validate formats, identify defects, compare against the brief, and isolate needed revisions.

04 / DELIVERY

Publish & document

Optimize assets, record their provenance, build usable showcases, and accurately separate complete work from planned concepts.
