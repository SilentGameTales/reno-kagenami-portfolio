The creative challenge

Build a consistent performer.

VEXA has a defined identity: a sleek black bob, a mature contralto sound, and mismatched eyes (anatomical right icy blue, left amber). Different camera positions, expressions, and wardrobe details were selected against this standard. The sonic brief similarly locks a low-register, fully sung performance rather than spoken rap.

The sound specification.

This is the production prompt used to communicate the requested vocal identity, groove, arrangement and exclusions. It records intent, not an assertion that every instruction was followed perfectly.

Dark melodic Brooklyn drill, mature deep soulful female contralto, smoky rich lower register, warm chest resonance, fully sung melodic drill flow, gritty emotional vocals, rhythmic pitch-driven delivery, haunting female harmonies, aggressive soaring vocal melodies, expressive melisma, sliding distorted 808s, hard syncopated drill drums, rapid hi-hat patterns, dark Rhodes piano, eerie bell textures, atmospheric synths, cinematic bass drops, sparse menacing production, powerful melodic hooks. Cold Brooklyn drill energy with emotional R&B undertones. Intimate low-register verses rising into wide sustained melodic choruses. Every lyric sung with clear pitch and expressive melody. No male vocals, spoken rap, talking, chanting, monotone delivery, or bright youthful pop vocals.

The Rhodes-piano direction is specific to this song, not a permanent rule for VEXA's music.
