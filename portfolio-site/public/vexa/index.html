VEXA / Before the Lights

Before the Lights

AI-generated musical performance: Mureka AI. Human creative direction and style specification: Kahlil.

Visual campaign / 9 approved images

One identity. Nine angles.

Rain-streaked glass, a dim apartment, and warm practical light unify a variety of portrait compositions.

Select an image to enlarge it. Artwork is AI-generated and creator-selected.

The creative challenge

Build a consistent performer.

VEXA has a defined identity: a sleek black bob, a mature contralto sound, and mismatched eyes (anatomical right icy blue, left amber). Different camera positions, expressions, and wardrobe details were selected against this standard. The sonic brief similarly locks a low-register, fully sung performance rather than spoken rap.

Direction / selection

Kahlil

Project concept, character continuity requirements, Mureka style specification, creative review, and final asset selection.

Lyrics / images

ChatGPT

Collaborative lyric development and AI generation of the cover and accompanying promotional images.

Music / vocals

Mureka AI

Generated the final music track and virtual singing from the detailed production brief.

Actual generation input / provenance

The sound specification.

This is the production prompt used to communicate the requested vocal identity, groove, arrangement and exclusions. It records intent, not an assertion that every instruction was followed perfectly.

Dark melodic Brooklyn drill, mature deep soulful female contralto, smoky rich lower register, warm chest resonance, fully sung melodic drill flow, gritty emotional vocals, rhythmic pitch-driven delivery, haunting female harmonies, aggressive soaring vocal melodies, expressive melisma, sliding distorted 808s, hard syncopated drill drums, rapid hi-hat patterns, dark Rhodes piano, eerie bell textures, atmospheric synths, cinematic bass drops, sparse menacing production, powerful melodic hooks. Cold Brooklyn drill energy with emotional R&B undertones. Intimate low-register verses rising into wide sustained melodic choruses. Every lyric sung with clear pitch and expressive melody. No male vocals, spoken rap, talking, chanting, monotone delivery, or bright youthful pop vocals.

The Rhodes-piano direction is specific to this song, not a permanent rule for VEXA's music.

Quality evaluation / evidence

What was checked.

Technical checks and human visual judgment are kept separate. No invented model-accuracy score or listener statistics.

Visual consistency

Bob silhouette, visible heterochromia, atmospheric lighting and selected compositions. Small accessories and anatomical precision still need close review.

Listening review

Contralto tone, melodic phrasing, chorus lift and mix clarity remain qualitative listening criteria, not numerically verified results.

Completed: a generated song, official cover, eight selected supporting images. Planned, not claimed as delivered: a finished music video, formal before/after comparison set, and repeatability measurements.
